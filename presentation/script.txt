Title slide (1 min)
- Open with the core idea: border access is not just distance; it is road-network access to an operational crossing.
- Say this talk studies the Colombia-Venezuela border because the 2019 restrictions created a sharp access shock.

Motivation (2 min)
- Explain why border closures matter for markets, suppliers, commuting, and informal exchange.
- Point out that two places equally close to the border can face different detours once roads and crossings are considered.

Introduction (2 min)
- State the research question clearly: how did changes in access to the border affect local economic activity?
- Emphasize the contribution: a continuous exposure measure and quarterly satellite data.

Conceptual framework (1.5 min)
- Walk through the mechanism: policy shock -> higher travel cost -> weaker local activity.
- Mention that the hypothesis is a negative relationship, but it must be interpreted carefully because pre-trends can exist.

Methodology (2 min)
- Describe the network-distance measure and how treatment intensity is built from pre/post access.
- Explain the fixed-effects ladder: baseline TWFE, then country-quarter, corridor-quarter, and state-quarter controls.

Data (2 min)
- Introduce the four data layers: municipalities, roads, crossings, and VIIRS nighttime lights.
- Use the nightlights rotation to show that the outcome is quarterly and spatially detailed.

Results: main estimates (2 min)
- Say the baseline effect is negative, but it shrinks as the controls become more localized.
- Stress that the tighter specifications make the estimate much less robust.

Results: dynamic evidence (2 min)
- Explain that the event study shows differential pre-trends.
- Say this is why the clean causal story is weaker than the raw TWFE estimate suggests.

Results: reopening and heterogeneity (2 min)
- Use the reopening figure as the strongest mechanism-consistent validation.
- Mention the country-specific differences, but avoid over-interpreting them.

Conclusion (1 min)
- End with the cautious takeaway: border access likely matters, but the evidence is strongest as supportive rather than definitive causal proof.
- If asked, mention the appendix has buffer robustness and extra event-study panels.

Landing slide
- Thank the audience and invite questions.
